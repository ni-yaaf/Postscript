A Little Room to Breathe

On feeling distant from the people you love.. Finding comfort in solitude.. And slowly reconnecting with the little things that make life yours...

Kiddo...

I don't think you're describing a lack of love for your family... In fact.. you make it quite clear that you still love them... What I think you're describing is how difficult it has become to actually be around them... Those are two very different things.. And I wish you didn't have to feel guilty for experiencing them separately...

You can love someone and still feel overwhelmed by their presence... You can miss spending time with your family and still find yourself wanting to be alone when the opportunity comes. You can appreciate that people care about you and still feel exhausted by being constantly noticed.. Questioned.. Or reminded of what you haven't done... None of those feelings cancel each other out...

And I think that's an important distinction in your post.. You don't seem to be saying that you want everyone out of your life... You seem to want a little room to exist without feeling like every part of your day is being observed and evaluated...

I can understand why that would make you retreat further into your own space... When even ordinary things become subjects of conversation.. Sometimes being alone feels like the only time you aren't expected to explain yourself... You don't have to justify why you're awake.. Why you're eating at a certain time.. Why you haven't gone outside.. Or why you haven't done something that day...

But I want you to be careful not to let that solitude become another thing that traps you...

There's a difference between choosing to spend some time alone because it gives you peace and gradually becoming so disconnected from everything that returning to it feels impossible... You deserve privacy.. Absolutely. But you also deserve moments where you feel comfortable being part of the world again.. Without having to force yourself into some version of normality...

You mentioned that you sometimes stay awake so you can eat without anyone watching or commenting... I understand the reasoning behind that.. But I don't want you to feel as though you have to rearrange your entire day just to make something as ordinary as eating feel comfortable... You deserve to eat when you're hungry.. Rest when you're tired.. And exist in your own home without feeling like you're doing something wrong...

Perhaps.. Instead of trying to change your entire routine at once.. You could start by making a few small parts of the day easier for yourself... Keep something simple to eat within reach... Open the curtains when you wake up... Sit somewhere outside your room for a little while.. Even if you don't feel like talking... You don't have to turn these things into a schedule or a list of obligations... They're simply small ways of giving yourself a little more space in your day...

And about your family.. Perhaps you could try explaining what you need in terms of specific actions rather than trying to explain everything you're feeling... Something as simple as.. "I know you're checking on me because you care.. But repeated reminders make me feel overwhelmed... Could you give me a little time and let me approach you when I'm ready?" You shouldn't have to find the perfect words every time.. But sometimes making the request specific can help people understand what would actually make things easier...

I also noticed what you said about your phone...

I don't think it's necessarily about being particularly interested in what's on your screen... Sometimes it's simply easier to have something occupying your attention than to sit in silence with a mind that won't leave you alone... Even when you're bored.. Scrolling can feel preferable to having nothing between you and your thoughts...

But I hope you can gradually find other little things that give you that same sense of comfort without demanding too much from you... Not as replacements for your phone.. And certainly not as another set of expectations... Just little things that belong to you...

You mentioned drawing.. Making paper stars.. And baking... I found that part particularly important because you haven't stopped caring about those things... You still miss them... You still want to do them... The difficulty is finding the energy to begin...

So perhaps don't approach them as hobbies you need to return to... Don't tell yourself that you need to finish a drawing.. Make a whole collection of stars.. Or bake something properly... If you feel like drawing.. Make one small mark on a page... If you want to make a paper star.. Make one... If you miss baking.. Even looking through a recipe can be enough for that moment...

You don't have to turn every little interest into a completed activity... You are allowed to enjoy something without being productive with it...

And I want you to stop measuring your days exclusively by what you managed to accomplish... A day doesn't become worthless because you didn't shower.. Go outside.. Finish something.. Or follow a particular routine... Those things matter.. Of course.. But they aren't a measurement of your character... You are not less deserving of patience on the days when you struggle to do them...

I think the hardest part of your post is the way you describe yourself as slowly slipping away from everything... It sounds as though you're watching yourself become more distant from the things and people that once made up your everyday life.. While simultaneously not having the energy to find your way back...

I don't want you to interpret that distance as proof that the old you is gone... You haven't lost the right to enjoy drawing just because you haven't drawn in a while... You haven't lost your place in your family because you've been spending more time alone... And you haven't become a different person simply because your days look different now...

You don't need to reconstruct your entire life overnight... You don't need to wake up tomorrow and suddenly become sociable.. Energetic.. Organised.. And enthusiastic about everything again... That would be an unreasonable expectation to place on yourself...

Start smaller... Give yourself permission to have imperfect days.. But try not to abandon yourself during them... Eat something when you can... Let a little daylight into your room... Do one thing that reminds you of something you used to enjoy... Spend a few minutes around someone you feel comfortable with.. Without demanding that you become conversational or cheerful...

And when you need space.. Take it without turning that need into a reason to punish yourself...

I hope you eventually find a way to feel at home around the people you love again.. Without feeling that you have to surrender your privacy to do so... You deserve both.. The freedom to have your own space and the comfort of knowing that you belong somewhere...

Please don't mistake a difficult chapter of your life for the entirety of who you are... There's still room for little things you enjoy.. For ordinary afternoons.. For conversations that don't feel exhausting.. And for days that don't feel quite so heavy...

You don't have to get all of that back at once. Just don't give up on the possibility of having it again...

End of letter.
